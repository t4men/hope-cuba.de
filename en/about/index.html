About us

Since his first trip to Cuba in January 2023, Christof Kluska (CK) has privately supported families in need in Cuba. From this experience, Hope Cuba is being created as a lasting aid project.

Our success factor: trust on the ground

Our Critical Success Factor (CSF) is personal, trustworthy contacts and helpers on the ground – not a formally registered partner organization.

Our commitment

We aim for evidence, independent review and responsible use of funds. We only report as fact what has actually been done.

Where we help

Our focus is Cuba. According to the draft articles, help for refugees and migrants in Germany and for people of other nationalities is also possible.

Association status
