Pilot project · Holguín, Cuba

Solar Aid Center Holguín

Planned solar-powered aid center with battery storage, refrigeration, safe supplies storage, lighting, water treatment and phone charging. The starting point is an existing house with a well in Holguín.

Project status

Initiative in formationHope Cuba is not yet registered or recognized as tax-exempt. We currently cannot issue tax-deductible donation receipts.

Background

Frequent power cuts make it hard to keep food and medicines cool and to provide everyday supplies.

Location

Holguín, Cuba. An existing house with a well is intended as the pilot site; ownership and usage rights are still to be clarified.

Planned features

✓

Photovoltaics and battery storage

✓

Refrigeration of food and – where medically suitable – temperature-sensitive medicines

✓

Safe storage of aid supplies

✓

Water treatment

✓

Lighting

✓

Phone charging station

✓

Possibly cooking facilities, subject to a realistic energy balance

Refrigeration of medicines, including insulin, only with reliable temperature monitoring and a qualified partner.

Development & funding

Planned: two solar panels, battery storage, two refrigerators, a water filter and public charging points. A soup kitchen may follow.

Estimated first-stage need: approximately USD 4,000. This is a planning estimate.
