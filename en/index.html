Humanitarian aid – starting in Holguín, Cuba

Hope begins with practical help.

We support people in need with food, essential medicines and sustainable community services. We start in Holguín and aim to expand where help is needed.

HOPE. HELP. FUTURE.

Practical help today. Sustainable opportunities tomorrow.

Many families in Cuba struggle every day for food, medicine and electricity. Hope Cuba works with trusted people on the ground whom we know personally, and makes it clear who receives which help and how funds are used.

Initiative in formationHope Cuba is not yet registered or recognized as tax-exempt. We currently cannot issue tax-deductible donation receipts.

Four programs

🥫

Food & Essential Medicines

Food, hygiene supplies and urgently needed medicines for families in need. Needs and distributions are documented; medicines are handled through lawful, qualified channels.

☀️

Solar Aid Center Holguín

Planned solar-powered aid center with battery storage, refrigeration, safe supplies storage, lighting, water treatment and phone charging. The starting point is an existing house with a well in Holguín.

🏥

Healthcare & Education

Support for health facilities, essential care and training. Later, education, school projects and vocational opportunities.

🤝

New Opportunities – Sponsor a New Life

Case-assessed support for people demonstrably in need: training, voluntary lawful relocation and support for a new start, where legally charitable. We also aim to support refugees and migrants in Germany regardless of nationality.

Pilot project

Solar Aid Center Holguín

According to the initiator, an existing house with a well in Holguín serves as the starting point. A solar-powered aid center is to be built there – step by step and only after technical review.

About the initiative

Since his first trip to Cuba in January 2023, Christof Kluska has privately supported families in need in Cuba. From this experience, Hope Cuba is growing into a lasting, accountable structure for help.

Frequently asked questions

What is Hope Cuba and where does the work begin?

Hope Cuba is an initiative in formation based in Messel, Germany. We help people in need with food, medicines and sustainable services – starting in Holguín, Cuba.

Is Hope Cuba already a registered charity?

Not yet. The association's formation is in progress; registration and tax recognition are still pending. We will update this page as soon as the status changes.

How do you make sure help arrives?

We work with trusted local people we know personally and document needs, purchasing, hand-over and use of funds.
