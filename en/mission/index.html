Our mission

Hope Cuba connects emergency relief with long-term opportunity. Our aim is to help people in need access food, essential medicines, reliable energy, education and new opportunities. We start with trusted local people in Holguín and make our work accountable through documented needs assessment, purchasing and use of funds.

The four pillars

🥫

Food & Essential Medicines

Food, hygiene supplies and urgently needed medicines for families in need. Needs and distributions are documented; medicines are handled through lawful, qualified channels.

☀️

Solar Aid Center Holguín

Planned solar-powered aid center with battery storage, refrigeration, safe supplies storage, lighting, water treatment and phone charging. The starting point is an existing house with a well in Holguín.

🏥

Healthcare & Education

Support for health facilities, essential care and training. Later, education, school projects and vocational opportunities.

🤝

New Opportunities – Sponsor a New Life

Case-assessed support for people demonstrably in need: training, voluntary lawful relocation and support for a new start, where legally charitable. We also aim to support refugees and migrants in Germany regardless of nationality.
